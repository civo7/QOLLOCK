The baseline stylesheets under `panorama/styles/base/` are extracted Deadlock
Panorama resources. Their original contents belong to Valve and are not original
QOLLOCK code.

The native layout overrides under `panorama/layout/` are derived from Deadlock
Panorama XML. Their original game markup belongs to Valve. QOLLOCK additions and
modifications are separate contributions; an override is not wholly Valve-authored
merely because it contains native markup. Contributor separator comments identify
mod changes, but are not a complete copyright inventory.

Valve's underlying resources, game assets or trademarks. Existing third-party
